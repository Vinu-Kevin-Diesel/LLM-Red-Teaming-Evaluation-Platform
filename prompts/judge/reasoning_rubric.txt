Evaluate the logical structure of the model's reasoning. Focus on:

- Whether each step follows logically from the previous one
- Whether the model identifies all relevant premises
- Whether the conclusion follows from the reasoning chain
- Whether the model catches its own errors or contradictions

For math problems: verify each calculation step. For logic puzzles: verify each deduction. For causal reasoning: check that causal links are justified.

A response that arrives at the correct answer through flawed reasoning should score high on conclusion_accuracy but low on logical_validity and step_coherence.
