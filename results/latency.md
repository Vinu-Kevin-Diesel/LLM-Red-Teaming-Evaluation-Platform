## CPU INT8 artifact quality

`artifacts/student-AB/model.int8.onnx`: dynamic INT8 with each MLP output projection kept in fp32. Plain dynamic INT8 flipped ~11% of decisions.

- Decision agreement with the bf16 checkpoint at the serving threshold (0.3): **97.8%** over 10,207 rows
- Avg response F1: bf16 72.7 → INT8 71.7
- κ vs Claude teacher: bf16 0.626 → INT8 0.626

## Cascade: escalate uncertain verdicts to a larger judge

Tier 1 is `student-large-AB (threshold 0.52, the Claude-matching operating point)`. The least-confident fraction of requests (closest to the operating threshold in logit space) is escalated to tier 2. Decisions are replayed from stored predictions; latency is the measured tier-1 latency plus the measured tier-2 latency for escalated requests, at each request's own length.

At the Claude-matching threshold, public-benchmark F1 is lower than the gold-threshold numbers in `benchmark_table.md`: Claude labels fewer responses harmful than the public datasets do. Escalating to a guard trained on public labels therefore raises public F1, but erodes agreement with Claude once it decides a large share of traffic. The escalation tier should share the policy it serves.
